Jade MCP uses the official @modelcontextprotocol/server and core packages
(Apache-2.0), Zod (MIT), and the Jade CLI runtime and owned native helpers.
Their package LICENSE files and notices are retained in the standalone bundle.

The Windows executable embeds Node.js v24.22.1. Its full license notice and
third-party licenses are included as NODE-LICENSE.txt in the distribution.

The CLI keeps its native runtime and vendored dependency notices under
node_modules/jade-tools-cli. Jade vendor DLLs are not distributed here.

postject is a build-only dependency under the MIT license and is not required
in the runtime distribution.
